Text display robustness update / 文字显示鲁棒性更新 — v0.7.2

- Added native 11/22/33px F3 fonts drawn at 1:1 with texture smoothing disabled.
- Built LanaPixel at its 11px design grid and generated exact 2x/3x nearest-neighbor variants; Chinese fallback glyphs retain antialiasing.
- Reflowed full bilingual conditions, expanded the panel height-first, and automatically downgraded oversized tiers.
- Separated HUD and F3 text settings; HUD now auto-fits and stays inside safe bounds.
- Migrated saved settings to schema 7 without overwriting position preferences.

- 新增关闭纹理平滑并按 1:1 绘制的 F3 原生 11/22/33px 字库。
- LanaPixel 以 11px 设计栅格生成，22/33px 为精确 2×/3× 最近邻版本；中文后备字形保留抗锯齿。
- 完整换行中英成就条件，面板先增高、再加宽，并在字号过大时自动降档。
- 分离 HUD 与 F3 字号设置；HUD 会自动缩小并保持在安全区域内。
- 存档升级到 schema 7，保留用户原始位置偏好。
