Text display robustness update / 文字显示鲁棒性更新 — v0.7.2

- Added shared native 11/22/33px HUD/F3 fonts drawn at 1:1 with texture smoothing disabled.
- Built LanaPixel at its 11px design grid and generated exact 2x/3x nearest-neighbor variants; Chinese fallback glyphs retain antialiasing.
- Reflowed full bilingual conditions, expanded the panel height-first, and automatically downgraded oversized tiers.
- HUD now downgrades by native tier, shifts left only at the 11px floor, and paginates complete goals on tiny screens.
- Added a 0–8px HUD line spacing slider; layout fitting, pagination, and centered warnings all honor it.
- Migrated HUD and F3 settings to schema 9 without overwriting position preferences.
- Global game, GPU, or display filtering may still soften the final image; the mod does not override Filter.

- 新增关闭纹理平滑并按 1:1 绘制的 HUD/F3 共享原生 11/22/33px 字库。
- LanaPixel 以 11px 设计栅格生成，22/33px 为精确 2×/3× 最近邻版本；中文后备字形保留抗锯齿。
- 完整换行中英成就条件，面板先增高、再加宽，并在字号过大时自动降档。
- HUD 按原生档位降档，仅在 11px 仍不足时左移，并在极小画面按完整目标分页。
- 新增 0–8px HUD 行间距滑块，布局适配、分页和居中警告均使用该设置。
- HUD 与 F3 设置升级到 schema 9，保留用户原始位置偏好。
- 游戏、显卡或显示器的全局滤波仍可能使最终画面变软；本 Mod 不覆盖 Filter 设置。
